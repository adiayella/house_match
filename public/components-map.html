public/assets/match.js		 The third profile is saved, or someone sends /match. Three profiles and the listing records. Equal three-way split; journeys checked at weekday office hours; missing fields treated as unknown. Splits the rent and checks each share against that person’s own ceiling; eliminates anything breaking a stated non-negotiable; scores preferences and travel; ranks on the worst-off person rather than the average. Two or three qualifying flats with a per-person breakdown — or an empty shortlist naming the limits that eliminated everything. Never a flat chosen for them.
GeminiLLM · lib/gemini.js Someone types a travel answer in their own words, or a ranked option needs writing up. The raw sentence at intake; afterwards, the verdict the engine has already reached. The closed destination list, and each person’s passes, failures and open questions. Reads a typed answer into a structured suggestion, asking rather than guessing when no time limit was given. Writes the tradeoff in plain English. Never decides. It cannot see the filtering and nothing it returns re-enters eligibility. A clarifying question, or two or three sentences naming who is compromising on what. If the key is absent the bot falls back to deterministic wording and matching is unaffected.
Property APIpublic/data/listings.js A match run begins. Area, address, BHK, rent, deposit, built-up area, furnishing, preferred tenants, availability. Scraped live from NoBroker on 27 Sep 2026; the RapidAPI wrapper does not work, so the rendered search page was read per area. The raw scrape is committed verbatim and normalised in visible code. Preserves missing fields as null rather than defaulting them. Lift, parking, bathrooms and maintenance are unstated for all 55 records, so absent data can never satisfy a requirement. Area labels contradicted by their own address are flagged, and travel checks on those records are downgraded rather than trusted. 55 real records across 6 Pune areas, each with its own NoBroker link so any row can be checked by hand.
SupabaseDatabase · lib/store.js Somebody answers a question, completes the form, or votes on an option. Profiles, in-progress form state, and interested / discuss / reject votes. Serverless functions keep nothing between requests, so all state lives here. Row level security is on with no public policy. Reads and writes over PostgREST using the service role. One participant cannot read another’s answers out of the database before the match runs. Durable profiles, resumable conversations and a vote tally. Without it the bot falls back to memory and says so on screen.
Other APIOptional · public/data/travel.js A listing has to be tested against somebody’s travel limit. The listing’s area, the destination, and that person’s maximum minutes. Kavita’s office run to Hinjewadi; Riya’s 25-minute radius to her family and her gym. Compares a journey range against the limit: upper bound inside it passes, lower bound already past it fails, and a range straddling the limit returns needs verification rather than being rounded to a midpoint and called a match. A pass, a fail or an open question per person per destination. These are area-level planning estimates; a live routing API would drop in here unchanged.

What the system will not do

Choose

It ranks and explains. The decision stays with the three of them, which is the whole point of handing them options with the compromises written down.

Quietly relax a dealbreaker

If nothing qualifies it says so and names the limits doing the eliminating. Filling the shortlist by softening somebody’s requirement would recreate the exact failure this replaces.

Treat missing data as a pass

An unstated lift is an open question attached to the option, never a satisfied requirement. Nobody is told a dealbreaker is met on the strength of a blank field.
